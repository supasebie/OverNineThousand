## The problem

Short-form video is the cheapest way for a small app, site or shop to get discovered, and the feed rewards people who post often, for weeks, without running out of ideas.

Every one of those posts is six jobs: an angle, the copy, the images, the video, a caption and a schedule. Then you do it again for each platform. Most makers, me included, post for a few days, get pulled back into the product, and lose all their momentum by week two.

I didn't want to hire an agency, and I didn't want to become a full-time content creator. So I built the tool I wanted.

## What PostQuake does

One link in, a week of posts out:

1. **Import your product.** Paste an App Store or Google Play link, or any website. PostQuake pulls in the name, pitch, icon and brand colours, and takes screenshots from the store listing or from your site itself.
2. **Learn who it's for.** A one-time, ten-question interview about what you've made, then research into real reviews and forum threads. The result is a customer profile written in your customers' own words, with every quote sourced. Nothing is made up.
3. **Write, check, render.** Pick your formats and how many posts you want. Every hook, slide and caption is written for that customer, checked against the format's rules and the rest of the batch, then rendered as 1080×1920 slides and an MP4 with licensed music.
4. **Ship it.** Export a ready-to-post bundle with a posting plan, or schedule directly to TikTok, Reels, Shorts and Facebook Pages. Direct scheduling is rolling out now.

## Honest marketing, on purpose

Psychic Tournament's design brief was honest odds. PostQuake's is honest marketing. Churning out slideshows is easy. The hard part is doing it every week without repeating yourself, getting your account flagged, or making claims you can't back up. So PostQuake has rules it won't break:

- **It never posts the same thing twice.** Every set is checked against its format and the rest of the batch before anything renders. Duplicate hooks and captions are rejected, and near-duplicates are flagged.
- **It won't write fake reviews.** Customer quotes have to be real and sourced. Mocked chats and notifications are labelled on the slide as dramatizations and never passed off as real users.
- **AI labels are handled for you.** Sets with AI imagery are labelled wherever the platform asks. Sets built from your own photos or licensed stock don't need one. Provenance is never stripped.
- **It uses licensed music only.** Videos use tracks with a recorded licence, and anything else is refused before it can get you muted.
- **It costs pennies per post.** AI images run about 10 cents each, and cached images cost nothing to re-render. Slides, videos, mock phone screens and store cards all render for free.

## What's coming

Here's where each piece stands:

- **Rolling out now:** direct scheduling across TikTok, Reels, Shorts and Facebook.
- **Next:** a web app, so you don't need a terminal. Also hook A/B variants, per-post attribution (installs, sign-ups or sales), and your own brand kit.
